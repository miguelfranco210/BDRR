Required compliance review

Required Acknowledgements

Before submitting, every volunteer must review and acknowledge requirements tied to the negotiated release of the Ridglan Farms beagles.

  • Keep non-public information confidential.
  • Do not make public criticism or comments about dog care, treatment, condition, handling, or negotiations.
  • No photos, video, or audio in the medical barn.
  • Follow biosecurity and Big Dog Ranch Rescue staff direction.
